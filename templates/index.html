Target Classes

Three-Tier Clinical Classification

Trained specifically on clinical oral mucosal photography to distinguish healthy tissue from dysplasia and invasive carcinoma.

Class 0

Normal Mucosa

Healthy oral mucous membrane without evidence of epithelial dysplasia, inflammatory hyperplasia, or neoplastic alterations.

Class 1

Pre-Cancer / Dysplasia

Potentially malignant oral disorders (PMOD) including leukoplakia, erythroplakia, and submucous fibrosis requiring monitoring.

Class 2

Oral Malignancy

Features consistent with oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC) exhibiting ulceration, rolled margins, and tissue induration.

Clinical & Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

OralVision AI utilizes a specialized DenseNet201 (Dense Convolutional Network with 201 layers) architecture. DenseNet connects each layer to every other layer in a feed-forward fashion, ensuring maximum information flow and minimizing feature vanishing.
Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) calculates the gradients of the classification score with respect to the final convolutional feature maps (conv5 block). It produces a visual heatmap highlighting the exact spatial regions of the lesion that influenced the AI decision.
OralVision implements a strict abstention rule: if the top predicted class has less than 60% confidence or the margin between the top two classes is under 10%, the system refrains from guessing and labels the output as "Inconclusive / Needs Review".
